Executive Summary - Government Scheme Fund Analytics

Dataset: Synthetic government fund utilization records (1,000 rows; financials, physical targets, beneficiaries).

Top findings:
- Average fund utilization by financial year ~68% (see reports/financial_year_summary.csv).
- Top-funded schemes: Jal Jeevan Mission; Swachh Bharat Mission - Gramin; National Health Mission; PM Kisan Samman Nidhi; PM Awas Gramin (see reports/top_schemes_by_sanctioned.csv).
- States with largest sanctioned totals: West Bengal, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Bihar (see reports/state_funding_summary.csv).
- Strong correlation between sanctioned and released amounts (Pearson ~0.93). Utilization ratio correlates with physical and beneficiary achievement (Spearman up to ~0.96).
- Outliers flagged: records with unusually high or zero utilization relative to releases; see reports/outliers.csv for reconciliation.
